## Summary

**TLDR:** EFF’s DOGE court win matters, but stopping one unlawful disclosure will mean little unless agencies face durable limits on repurposing citizens’ data.

- A court ruled that OPM unlawfully disclosed federal workers’ confidential personnel records to DOGE, according to EFF’s account of its lawsuit.
- EFF presents the case as part of a broader fight against government agencies pooling personal information for purposes people never authorized.
- The newsletter also highlights Privacy Badger for Safari and a campaign supporting net neutrality in California.
- The ruling is an important check, but its practical value depends on remedies and whether it constrains future interagency data sharing.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** EFF 對 DOGE 資料調用取得的法院勝利值得肯定，但若政府機關仍可任意改用民眾資料，一次判決很難形成長久保障。

- 依 EFF 對訴訟結果的說明，法院判定美國人事管理局向 DOGE 揭露聯邦員工機密人事資料的行為違法。
- EFF 將此案放在更大的政府資料整併問題中，質疑機關把民眾為特定目的提交的資訊轉作其他用途。
- 本期電子報也介紹 Safari 版 Privacy Badger，並號召讀者支持加州的網路中立政策。
- 判決雖然設下界線，真正效果仍取決於法院救濟，以及它能否限制未來的跨機關資料共享。
