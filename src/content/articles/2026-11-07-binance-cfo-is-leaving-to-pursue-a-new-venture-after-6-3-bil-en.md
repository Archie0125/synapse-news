Binance’s chief financial officer Kaiser Ng is leaving the cryptocurrency exchange.

[According to Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/binance-says-kaiser-ng-leaving-names-justin-yek-new-cfo), co-CEO Yi He said in a statement that Ng’s departure will allow him to “pursue a new venture after more than five years at Binance,” and that a transition plan has been “implemented over the last half year.”

The announcement comes barely more than a week after [hackers stole $6.3 billion worth of cryptocurrency](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/01/binance-resumes-withdrawals-following-6-3-billion-breach/) from Binance users. The company froze withdrawals for several days; after accounts were unfrozen, users withdrew a net $3.6 billion in the first few hours.

Binance and [the FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/alerts/2026/north-korea-responsible-for-6-billion-binance-attack) blamed a North Korean group called TraderTraitor for the hack. The company also said that it would cover all losses, promising, “Binance will bear the financial impact of the stolen funds. These losses will not be passed on to users.”

Ng will be replaced as CFO by Justin Yek.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Binance says its CFO's exit was planned for six months, but announcing it a week after a $6.3 billion hack makes that hard to take at face value.

- Binance CFO Kaiser Ng is leaving after more than five years, with co-CEO Yi He saying a transition plan was implemented over the last half year.
- The announcement came barely a week after hackers stole $6.3 billion in cryptocurrency from Binance users.
- Binance and the FBI attributed the attack to North Korean group TraderTraitor.
- After a multi-day withdrawal freeze, users pulled a net $3.6 billion within hours of accounts reopening.
- Binance pledged to absorb all losses, which puts the new CFO, Justin Yek, in charge of a balance sheet under heavy pressure.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Binance 說財務長離職已規劃半年，但在 63 億美元駭客事件後一週宣布，這說法很難讓人照單全收。

- Binance 財務長 Kaiser Ng 在任職五年多後離開，共同執行長何一表示交接計畫已在過去半年執行。
- 消息公布時，距離駭客從 Binance 使用者手中盜走 63 億美元加密貨幣才一週多。
- Binance 和 FBI 都將攻擊歸咎於北韓駭客組織 TraderTraitor。
- 凍結提領數天後，帳戶一解凍，使用者在幾小時內淨提領 36 億美元。
- Binance 承諾吸收全部損失，新任財務長 Justin Yek 接手的是一份壓力沉重的資產負債表。
