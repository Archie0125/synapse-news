Crusoe, an eight-year-old, Denver-based data center developer recently valued at [$30.9 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/crusoe-raises-3-9b-to-build-massive-data-centers-and-small-modular-ai-factories/), [confirmed](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-wyoming-data-center-campus-update) Friday it has sold an unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google.

Google, which Crusoe had tried and failed to land as a customer for the project, is taking over the development after reportedly raising concerns about its cost and construction timeline under Crusoe’s management.

Crusoe initially announced the project last year with Blackstone-backed infrastructure company Tallgrass, planning to eventually scale it to 10 gigawatts of power capacity.

Google did not disclose how much it paid Crusoe for the unfinished project, but it announced plans to [invest $17 billion](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/wyoming-data-center/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) to develop the data center campus.

The deal highlights how difficult it can be for independent data center developers to deliver massive projects on time and within budget, given the challenges of securing equipment and power. Growing opposition from local communities can further complicate development.

Despite taking over the Wyoming facility, Google hasn’t walked away from working with Crusoe entirely. Crusoe is developing [another data](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-developing-data-center-campus-in-armstrong-county-texas) center for Google near Amarillo, Texas.

The deal to sell the site to Google was first reported [by Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/crusoe-pushed-aside-at-wyoming-ai-project-after-google-concerns) in June, but the Wyoming setback didn’t stop Crusoe from raising a [$3.9 billion round](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/crusoe-raises-3-9b-to-build-massive-data-centers-and-small-modular-ai-factories/) in September.

Crusoe makes money by leasing data center space to customers that bring their own GPUs, by renting out its own GPUs, and by selling compute power used to run AI models, known as inference.

Crusoe’s largest projects serve some of the biggest names in AI. Its campus in Abilene, Texas, is being built in phases for Oracle and OpenAI, and the company is also constructing a separate 900-megawatt campus next door for Microsoft, with its first building expected to come online in mid-2027. Meta is also a customer, and Bloomberg reported that the trading firm Jane Street signed a [$13 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/crusoe-signs-roughly-13-billion-ai-cloud-deal-with-jane-street), five-year cloud deal with Crusoe.

Crusoe was founded by Chase Lochmiller, now its CEO, and Cully Cavness, who started it as a cryptocurrency-mining business powered by natural gas that would otherwise have been burned off. In September, alongside its $3.9 billion funding round, the company added [three board members](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-appoints-seifert-stein-straubel-board-of-directors), including JB Straubel, the Redwood Materials founder and Tesla board member who invested in Crusoe in 2021. Crusoe later became the first customer of Straubel’s energy-storage business.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Crusoe handing its stalled Wyoming megaproject to Google proves that in the AI buildout, the money goes to whoever can deliver power and steel.

- Crusoe, recently valued at $30.9 billion, sold its unfinished southeastern Wyoming data center project to Google for an undisclosed price.
- Google had reportedly raised concerns about cost and construction timelines under Crusoe's management, after Crusoe failed to sign it as a customer.
- Google plans to invest $17 billion in the campus, which was originally planned with Tallgrass to scale to 10 gigawatts.
- The deal shows how hard it is for independent developers to secure equipment, power, and local approval at hyperscale.
- Crusoe still builds a separate data center for Google near Amarillo, Texas, so the two companies are still working together.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Crusoe 把卡關的懷俄明州超大型專案交給 Google，說明在 AI 基建競賽裡，真正吃得開的是能交出電力和鋼骨的人。

- 估值 309 億美元的 Crusoe，已把懷俄明州東南部尚未完工的資料中心專案賣給 Google，金額未公開。
- Crusoe 原本想拉 Google 當客戶卻沒談成，據報 Google 還對 Crusoe 主導下的成本與工期有疑慮。
- Google 將投入 170 億美元開發該園區；這個專案原本由 Crusoe 與 Tallgrass 合作，目標規模 10 GW。
- 獨立資料中心開發商要在超大規模下搞定設備、電力與地方支持，難度遠比募資高。
- 雙方沒有翻臉：Crusoe 仍在德州 Amarillo 附近替 Google 興建另一座資料中心。
