## Summary

**TLDR:** Black Friday has grown into a two-month season, and that sprawl helps retailers squeeze out urgency far more than it helps shoppers find real discounts.

- WIRED's Reviews team says it only lists discounts on gadgets it has tested and would recommend, and it tracks prices all year.
- Early deals now start before November, after Black Friday already spread into Cyber Monday and Cyber Week.
- Featured items include Google Pixel 11 phones, Pixel earbuds, Apple AirTags and AirPods Max 2.
- WIRED admits some 'lowest prices', such as the AirTag's, come around often, so a discount alone isn't a reason to buy now.
