The **latest 13-inch MacBook Air** is currently available for $1,099.99, marking the second time we’ve seen this $200 discount following the company’s [hefty price increases over the summer](/tech/952162/apple-price-increase-ram-shortage). Although the base model — which features Apple’s M5 processor, 16GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage — often sold for around $950 to $1,000 shortly after it launched in May, savings have been rare since July, making the current deal worth flagging. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GR1J6T45?tag=theverge02-20), [Best Buy](https://bestbuy.7tiv.net/c/482924/614286/10014?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bestbuy.com%2Fproduct%2Fapple-macbook-air-13-inch-laptop-m5-chip-with-10-core-cpu-and-8-core-gpu-16gb-memory-512gb-ssd-midnight%2FJJGCQLKXL7%2Fsku%2F6397846), and [B&H Photo](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=87443X1797501&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bhphotovideo.com%2Fc%2Fproduct%2F1956909-REG%2Fapple_mdha4ll_a_13_macbook_air_m5.html%2F) are all honoring the price across all color options.

# Apple’s M5 MacBook Air is down to its lowest price since July

You can get a temporary break from the $200 price hike that hit both Air sizes this summer.

If you’d prefer something with a larger screen, a six-speaker array instead of four, and slightly more power (with two additional GPU cores in the M5), **Apple’s 15-inch MacBook Air** is also on sale for $200 off. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GR1DVSQP?tag=theverge02-20), [Best Buy](https://bestbuy.7tiv.net/c/482924/614286/10014?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bestbuy.com%2Fproduct%2Fapple-macbook-air-15-inch-laptop-m5-chip-with-10-core-cpu-and-10-core-gpu-16gb-memory-512gb-ssd-midnight%2FJJGCQLKQL9), and [B&H Photo](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=87443X1797501&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bhphotovideo.com%2Fc%2Fproduct%2F1956927-REG%2Fapple_mdvq4ll_a_15_macbook_air_m5.html) are each selling the 16GB RAM / 512GB SSD configuration in most colors for $1,299.99.

Although it’s pricier than the newer MacBook Neo, the Air offers better value in numerous ways. It’s an ideal choice if you want faster performance, as it offers double the RAM and a speedier CPU with more cores. You also get twice as much storage, better speakers, a backlit keyboard, and a larger, higher-resolution screen. Given said differences, it’s easy to see how the Air’s improvements might make it worth the extra cost for some people.

If you’re still on the fence, though, I’d recommend reading my colleague Antonio G. Di Benedetto’s reviews, both of which were published before the summer price increases.

## Summary

**TLDR:** A $200 MacBook Air discount sounds generous, but it only undoes Apple's summer price hike. It's a deal only compared with the new, higher price.

- The 13-inch M5 MacBook Air with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage is $1,099.99 at Amazon, Best Buy and B&H Photo, $200 off.
- This is only the second time the discount has appeared since Apple's summer price increases, which The Verge links to a RAM shortage.
- Shortly after its May launch, the same model often sold for about $950 to $1,000.
- The 15-inch M5 MacBook Air, which adds two GPU cores and a six-speaker array, is also $200 off.
- Memory supply constraints are pushing up laptop prices, so current discounts are relative to a higher baseline.
