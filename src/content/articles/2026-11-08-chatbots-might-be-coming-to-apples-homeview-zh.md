Code in the iOS 27.3 beta suggests that Apple could add support for chatbots to its smart home hub. There’s a flag listed for a “Conversational Apps” category. While there are no details about what apps would fall under that heading, it seems reasonable that AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Claude could be added to HomeView. According to [pdfu](https://x.com/itspdfu/status/2118167522130178220) on X, that new beta also adds support for bidirectional audio routing and session management, but says it’s “still not fully wired up.”

# Chatbots might be coming to Apple’s HomeView

Code in iOS 27.3 has a flag for ‘Conversational Apps.’

Apple has also added [anti-spoofing](https://x.com/itspdfu/status/2118048744247570777) protection that will require a user to authenticate themselves with an iPhone after three failed attempts. It will also have a lockout system with increasingly longer wait times, starting at one hour for the second lockout, and climbing to eight for the fourth.

Neither feature has been officially announced by Apple, and there’s no word on when they might become available.

## Summary

**TLDR:** A Conversational Apps flag in iOS 27.3 code hints Apple may let third-party chatbots into HomeView, a sign it is opening the smart home hub rather than relying only on Siri.

- The iOS 27.3 beta includes a flag for a Conversational Apps category in HomeView, Apple's smart home hub.
- Chatbots such as ChatGPT and Claude could plausibly fall under that category, though Apple has given no details.
- The beta reportedly adds bidirectional audio routing and session management, which are described as not fully wired up yet.
- New anti-spoofing protection requires iPhone authentication after three failed attempts, with lockouts growing from one hour to eight hours.
- Apple has not officially announced either feature or given a release date.
