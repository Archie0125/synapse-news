I’ve been a self-employed contractor for about a decade now, and I’m bummed about [Harvest](https://www.getharvest.com/).

Harvest was a time-tracking and invoicing service. You’d track how many hours or weeks you spent on a project, put together an invoice, email it to the client, and close it out when they paid. There was a little Stripe integration for accepting credit and ACH payments on the invoice web view. A historical chart of total billed/paid amounts over time, and a table of invoices. Harvest was exactly what you want from small-business software: it solved a well-defined problem with a reliable, simple interface, and did so at a reasonable monthly price. They managed to avoid scope creep and never tried to become an accounting platform, or a blockchain for washing machines.<sup>[1](#fn-1)</sup> I loved Harvest and raved about it to every self-employed person I met.

Back in 2025 Harvest got bought out by [Bending Spoons](https://www.reddit.com/r/HarvestApp/comments/1q25xpy/purchase_by_bending_spoons/), the company that [gutted Vimeo](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46707699). I was worried they might ruin it, but was busy with other things and didn’t have time to look for new invoicing software.

Long story short, they raised my price from $13.32 to $133.98 per month by moving everyone to a new “enterprise” plan and “unlimited” usage billing model, which is, as far as I can tell, exactly what I had before. There’s also a “flex” plan which costs $12.65 per month… but that plan now skims an additional 2% on top of the usual \~3% card transaction fees. To be clear, that 3% pays for the whole credit card network, plus all the people working at Stripe and Mastercard, plus the financial risk of chargebacks and fraud, etc. Harvest isn’t doing any of that work. I don’t know about you, but I don’t think I need to spend 2% of my gross revenue to send a couple PDFs a month and wrap a Stripe web form.

I wrote to Harvest about this, and discovered that they don’t read customer emails any more. Instead they have a Large Language Model repeatedly vomit thousands of words at you. I’m no stranger to enshittification, but man, I did not anticipate the speed at which Bending Spoons would drive a perfectly good company into the ground.

Time to find a new invoicing service, I guess. Maybe bookkeeping too—Intuit’s Quickbooks Online seems to have completely jumped the shark. Any of y’all freelancers have a system you like?

Sorry to hear this—it was such a quality service. I used to used Harvest, but changed many years back to Cushion when I was still freelancing.

Has the same core set of features (including invoicing integrated with Stripe, but with no markup!), plus a number of others designed specifically with freelancers in mind. Kind of a one-man shop, but dependably so—Jonnie always responded quickly to support issues when they came up.

Really quality piece of software, full of delightful little bits, and felt much better supporting someone I could put a face to than a faceless company.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Bending Spoons' 10x price hike on Harvest is the private-equity playbook in plain sight: buy beloved simple software, then charge loyal users whatever they'll tolerate.

- Bending Spoons, which previously gutted Vimeo, acquired the time-tracking and invoicing tool Harvest in 2025.
- A long-time freelancer's price jumped from $13.32 to $133.98 a month after a move to a new 'enterprise' plan with features they say are unchanged.
- The cheaper $12.65 'flex' plan takes an extra 2% on top of the usual ~3% card fees, effectively taxing users' gross revenue.
- Harvest was loved because it stayed simple and focused instead of trying to become an accounting platform.
- The case adds to growing distrust of acquirers that buy stable SaaS products and squeeze locked-in customers.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Bending Spoons 把 Harvest 漲價 10 倍，正是赤裸裸的收購套路：買下深受喜愛的簡單軟體，再向死忠用戶榨出他們能忍受的最高價。

- 曾經大砍 Vimeo 的 Bending Spoons，在 2025 年收購了工時追蹤與開立發票工具 Harvest。
- 一位長期使用的自由工作者被轉到新的「企業」方案後，月費從 13.32 美元暴漲到 133.98 美元，但他認為功能和以前一樣。
- 較便宜的 12.65 美元「彈性」方案，則在原本約 3% 的刷卡手續費之外再抽 2%，等於直接從營收抽成。
- Harvest 受歡迎的原因正是它保持簡單專注，沒有硬要變成會計平台。
- 這起事件加深了外界對收購者的不信任：買下穩定的 SaaS 產品，再壓榨被綁住的客戶。
