Tesla is expanding its robotaxi service to another Texas city as it seeks to compete with Waymo and position itself as more than just an automaker. The company said in a [post on X](https://x.com/robotaxi/status/2119678179963187564?a=HvjRKSnlAHPRAN-3wpU&s=20) that it will make its robotaxis available in San Antonio starting at 5 a.m. local time on Monday.

Tesla didn’t provide any additional details about the service, such as the hours of operation, whether the fleet will offer customers its gold, two-seater Cybercab as well as its modified Model Y vehicles, or if these vehicles will have human safety monitors on board. But if the company follows its playbook of previous launches, it will start with a limited number of vehicles and a confined service area, and expand slowly from there.

Tesla CEO Elon Musk for a decade promised his company would deliver autonomous vehicles, and it eventually launched a [robotaxi service](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/22/tesla-launches-robotaxi-rides-in-austin-with-big-promises-and-unanswered-questions/) in Austin in June 2025. The launch, though limited and supported by human safety operators on board, was an important milestone for the company and a test of whether self-driving cars could be safely deployed using just cameras and end-to-end AI.

Since then, Tesla has slowly expanded. In January, the company removed safety monitors from its robotaxis in Austin, officially kicking off a true driverless service, and has spent the last 11 months expanding within the city, and to new cities in Florida and Texas. It now operates robotaxis in limited areas of Miami and Orlando as well as [Dallas](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/18/tesla-brings-its-robotaxi-service-to-dallas-and-houston/), Fort Worth, and Houston.

Its rival Waymo also operates a robotaxi service using a mix of driverless Jaguar I-Pace and Zeekr RT vehicles, branded Ojai, in Austin, Dallas, Houston, and San Antonio. The company, a subsidiary of Alphabet, recently [restarted services](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/waymo-restarts-san-antonio-service-five-months-after-flooding-troubles/) in San Antonio after a five-month pause following incidents in which its vehicles got stuck after entering flooded roadways.

While Tesla has more than 400 autonomous vehicles registered in its Austin fleet, its fleets in other cities have numbered between a handful and a few dozen, according to Texas vehicle registration data and the [Robotaxi Tracker website](https://robotaxitracker.com/). For example, Tesla has just 24 vehicles in Fort Worth and five in Miami, according to the tracker.

Tesla also has a ride-hailing service, under a transportation charter permit, that it operates in the San Francisco Bay Area. While the vehicles are often described as robotaxis, they are operated by a human behind the wheel. Tesla currently doesn’t have the required permits to operate a driverless ride-hailing service in California.

In spite of this expansion push, the company released data this summer that showed its robotaxi network drove fewer miles with paying customers in the second quarter than it did in the first, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/teslas-robotaxis-are-moving-in-reverse/) in July.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Tesla adding San Antonio sounds like momentum, but announcing a launch with no hours, fleet size or safety details means we still can't judge it.

- Tesla's robotaxi service launches in San Antonio on Monday at 5 a.m., announced only via a post on X.
- No details were given on hours, service area, whether the Cybercab is included, or whether safety monitors ride along.
- Tesla now runs limited service in Austin, Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Miami and Orlando, and removed safety monitors in Austin in January.
- Waymo already operates in San Antonio, so this is a direct head-to-head in a city where the rival got there first.
- The camera-only, end-to-end AI approach is still the core bet, and each new city expands the test without showing how it is scaling.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Tesla 把 Robotaxi 開進聖安東尼奧，看似攻勢不停，但營運時段、車隊規模、安全細節一概不提，這種擴張還無從評估。

- Tesla 只在 X 上發一則貼文，宣布週一清晨 5 點在聖安東尼奧啟動 Robotaxi 服務。
- 營運時段、服務範圍、會不會派出 Cybercab、車上有沒有安全監督員，全都沒交代。
- 目前 Tesla 已在奧斯汀、達拉斯、沃斯堡、休士頓、邁阿密與奧蘭多提供有限服務，奧斯汀今年 1 月起取消安全監督員。
- Waymo 早已在聖安東尼奧營運，Tesla 這次是正面搶進對手已經站穩的市場。
- 純視覺加端到端人工智慧仍是 Tesla 的核心賭注，城市越開越多，規模化的實際數據卻依舊不透明。
