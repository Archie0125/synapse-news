Asked about AI executives’ warnings about [their models’ safety](/ai-artificial-intelligence/996563/ai-safety-research-metr-redwood-openai-anthropic) on [*60 Minutes*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg), Trump said, “I think that the risk is not nearly as real as it– as they talk, and I think the risk can be managed, and it’s managed.” He claimed the DOJ is managing that risk, saying, “We had a situation where somebody was reported to us to be doing something bad with AI. We called them very strongly and said, ‘Get ready to go to jail. Please give your wife a kiss good night.’”

# Trump’s 60 Minutes interview touched on AI safety, threat to arrest an AI exec

Trump claims the DOJ told a top AI executive, ‘get ready to go to jail.’

Trump refused to name the person or company he was referring to, but said it was the leader of an AI company, “One of the big ones.” He claimed that after the threat of arrest, “this person totally changed everything.” Despite previously claiming that “artificial intelligence” is a term used by people he considers the enemy, Trump said AI rather than SI or [super intelligence](/policy/1002468/trump-ai-superintelligence-executive-order-ai) multiple times during the interview. Eventually he said China’s Xi is “…all in to SI, as I call it, because it really is super intelligence. It’s a much better name, I think, because it’s not artificial.”

*60 Minutes*’s Ross Douthat went on to ask if Trump would work with Democrats on AI safety, and Trump said he “absolutely would,” adding, “I don’t mind guardrails, but I don’t want to put us in a deep second position. We’re leading number one. And China has no guardrails, by the way. \[...\] They have the guardrails that whatever President Xi says goes. With me, it’s pretty strong, but it’s not quite that strong.”

## Summary

**TLDR:** Trump's idea of AI safety policy is apparently threatening an unnamed CEO with jail, which is governance by intimidation, not by rules.

- On 60 Minutes, Trump said AI risk is not as real as executives claim and that it is being managed, partly by the DOJ.
- He said officials told the leader of 'one of the big' AI companies to 'get ready to go to jail,' and that the person then 'totally changed everything.' He did not name the person or company.
- He said he would work with Democrats on AI safety and doesn't mind guardrails, but won't let the US fall to a 'deep second position' behind China.
- He prefers the term 'SI' (super intelligence) and claimed Xi is 'all in' on it.
- An unnamed, unverifiable threat is not a framework. It leaves AI companies guessing at the rules and exposed to political pressure.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** 川普的 AI 安全政策，看來就是威脅一位不具名的執行長要坐牢。這是靠恐嚇治理，不是靠規則。

- 川普在《60 Minutes》表示，AI 風險沒有業界說得那麼嚴重，而且已經受到控管，司法部也參與其中。
- 他說官方曾警告「某家大型」AI 公司的負責人「準備去坐牢吧」，對方之後「徹底改變了一切」，但他拒絕透露人名和公司。
- 他表示願意和民主黨合作處理 AI 安全，也不反對設護欄，但不能讓美國落到「遠遠第二」、輸給中國。
- 他偏好用「SI」（超級智慧）這個說法，並稱習近平在這方面「全力投入」。
- 一個不具名、無從查證的威脅，稱不上政策框架。AI 公司只能猜測規則，也更容易受到政治施壓。
