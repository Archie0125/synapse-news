Uber announced the launch of Uber Parking, a native in-app reservation feature that allows drivers to find, book, and pay for garage and lot spaces ahead of time. The new feature is a result of [Uber’s recent acquisition of SpotHero](/transportation/883344/uber-spothero-parking-reservation-acquisition), integrating a network of over 13,000 facilities across more than 400 cities into the company’s platform.

# Uber wants to park your car now, too

Powered by its SpotHero acquisition, Uber’s new parking reservation tool is a play for commuter loyalty — and a staging ground for its robotaxi future.

The new feature works by tapping the new “Parking” icon in the Uber app to view an interactive map with upfront prices and arrival times. You can filter spots by self-park or valet, outdoor lot or garage, and whether staff are on-site. The app will prompt you to enter your vehicle details and license plate, after which can enter certain lots using a QR code on your phone or through an automatic license plate scan. If your plans change, you can extend your time directly in the app or cancel for free right up until your reservation starts.

[Uber One subscribers](/2020/8/18/21374105/uber-ride-pass-subscription-cities-us-price-food-delivery) can earn 10 percent back on parking reservations as account credits within 24 hours. These credits can then be spent on rides or Uber Eats deliveries. Uber says that its subscription service has grown to more than 55 million members across 47 countries as of late 2026.

Uber Parking is launching in 15 initial markets, including Chicago, NYC, Boston, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Washington D.C., Atlanta, Austin, Dallas, Denver, Miami, Nashville, Philadelphia, San Diego, and Seattle — eventually rolling out to SpotHero’s existing 400-city footprint.

The move also ties directly into [Uber’s wider bet on robotaxis](/news/935204/uber-av-lab-self-driving-data-collection), working with over 30 autonomous vehicle partners across multiple cities. In the long run, these 13,000-plus parking facilities won’t just hold commuter cars — Uber plans to turn them into urban holding pens and waiting areas for driverless robotaxis.

Stashing autonomous fleets off the street when demand dips helps keep empty cars from aimlessly circling city blocks, cutting down on traffic, emissions, and wear on the vehicles, Uber says. Ultimately, it shows that the company is thinking bigger than just ride-hailing and delivery — it wants to control the city’s physical roads and parking spaces, regardless of whether the person traveling is driving their own car, riding in the backseat, or letting a computer take the wheel.

By getting into parking, Uber is also tacitly admitting that its earlier mission to [end personal car ownership and driver’s licenses](https://fortune.com/2026/08/31/uber-president-coo-andrew-macdonald-future-with-autonomous-vehicles-no-car-ownership-or-drivers-licenses-career-success/) didn’t succeed. But there is a loyalty-retaining strategy at play here, too, with Uber intent on keeping its customers operating within in its own platform ecosystem, whether they’re hailing a ride or driving their own car.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Uber Parking looks like a commuter convenience, but the bigger play is owning the garages that robotaxi fleets will need.

- Uber Parking lets users find, book, and pay for garage and lot spaces in the app, built on its SpotHero acquisition covering 13,000+ facilities in 400+ cities.
- It launches in 15 US markets, including NYC, Chicago, and San Francisco, with QR or license-plate entry and free cancellation before the reservation starts.
- Uber One members get 10% back as credits for rides or Eats; Uber One has 55 million+ members across 47 countries.
- Uber now serves people who drive their own cars, not just people who ride, which widens its reach in daily trips.
- Uber works with 30+ autonomous vehicle partners, and parking networks could become depots for robotaxi fleets.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Uber Parking 表面上是通勤族的便利功能，真正的布局是先拿下 Robotaxi 車隊未來需要的停車場。

- Uber Parking 讓使用者在 App 內搜尋、預訂並支付停車位，背後是收購 SpotHero 得來的網絡，涵蓋 400 多座城市、超過 13,000 個停車場。
- 首波在紐約、芝加哥、舊金山等 15 個美國市場上線，可用 QR Code 或車牌辨識入場，預約開始前都能免費取消。
- Uber One 會員可拿回 10% 回饋，用在叫車或 Uber Eats；Uber One 會員已超過 5,500 萬人，遍及 47 個國家。
- Uber 現在連自己開車的人也一起服務，不只服務叫車乘客，在日常移動中的觸角更廣。
- Uber 已和 30 多家自駕車業者合作，停車網絡未來可能成為 Robotaxi 車隊的調度據點。
