Launching a major tech IPO would seem risky right now, shortly after one of Nasdaq’s biggest sell-offs and amid revelations that a CEO central to the AI boom had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. But that isn’t deterring Anthropic, which is moving ahead with its listing plans, according to [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/anthropic-prepares-to-push-ahead-with-ipo-despite-market-jitters-843dd16c).

Anthropic plans to set a valuation range this week and begin trading on Thursday or Friday of next week, the Journal reported.

Investors will look to Anthropic’s valuation to gauge the strength of the AI boom. Some institutional investors are now pushing for a valuation below the $1.5 trillion target discussed as recently as last week, a figure that was itself considerably below the $2 trillion floated a few months ago, the report said.

OpenAI, Anthropic’s main rival, was reportedly valued at [$1.25 trillion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/) last week after the ChatGPT maker raised $11 billion in fresh capital, short of the $20 billion it had initially sought.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Anthropic is reportedly pushing ahead with its IPO into a shaky market, and a valuation target that keeps sliding is the clearest read yet on where AI enthusiasm stands.

- Per The Wall Street Journal, Anthropic plans to set a valuation range this week and begin trading as soon as Thursday or Friday of next week.
- Some institutional investors are pushing for less than the $1.5 trillion target discussed last week, which was already well below the $2 trillion floated months ago.
- The timing is risky: it comes shortly after one of Nasdaq's biggest sell-offs and amid a reported scandal involving a CEO central to the AI boom.
- Rival OpenAI was reportedly valued at $1.25 trillion after raising $11 billion, short of the $20 billion it initially sought.
- The IPO will be treated as a public test of the AI boom, and the final price will matter well beyond Anthropic.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** 據報 Anthropic 頂著動盪市場硬推 IPO，而一路下修的估值目標，是目前衡量 AI 熱度最直接的溫度計。

- 根據《華爾街日報》，Anthropic 計畫本週訂出估值區間，最快下週四或週五掛牌交易。
- 部分機構投資人希望估值低於上週討論的 1.5 兆美元，而這個數字已經比幾個月前傳出的 2 兆美元低不少。
- 時機不太好：那斯達克才剛經歷一波大型拋售，同時傳出 AI 熱潮中的核心執行長捲入醜聞。
- 對手 OpenAI 據報募得 110 億美元、估值 1.25 兆美元，但募資額低於原先目標的 200 億美元。
- 這次 IPO 會被當成 AI 熱潮的公開檢驗，最終定價的影響遠不只 Anthropic 一家。
