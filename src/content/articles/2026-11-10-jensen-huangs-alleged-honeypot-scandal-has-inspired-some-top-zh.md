Jensen Huang, the CEO and co-founder of the world’s most prominent chip company, has allegedly been honey-trapped by a suspected Chinese spy, and the news has struck the internet like a match to gasoline.

Huang has long been seen as a wife guy (or so everyone assumed). He and his wife, Lori, have been together since the 1980s, and both of their children work at Nvidia. Huang has been the closest thing the tech world has to a rock star, with fans lining up for selfies and autographs. Of all the AI overlords, the leather-jacket wearing Huang has seemed like the most level-headed and least erratic, which is why [this plot twist has hit so hard.](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-puts-nvidias-china-ambitions-under-scrutiny/)

The potential fallout is mind-boggling. Nvidia looms so large over the U.S. stock market that even the company’s domestic drama is a market risk. Countless AI startups depend on Nvidia chips, so any shake-up at the top would ripple far beyond the company. And Nvidia has no publicly disclosed succession plan, so if Huang leaves, it’s unclear who’d take over.

Is *this* how the AI bubble bursts? It’s a question that will be answered in time, but in the meantime, a more pressing matter demands our attention: ranking the funniest memes about the scandal, with bonus points for AI images presumably generated using Nvidia chips.

There’s admittedly a lot to choose from, ranging from “500 Days of Summer” [jokes](https://x.com/tQuietEngineer/status/2119687115428798784?a=UPwqft7Sh5oV-vnVLN8&s=20) to jokes about [divorce lawyers](https://x.com/alifarhat79/status/2119814306963284126?a=pHd0M5BXT6eq2Zt8oeE&s=20) to an [AI-adaptation of the music video for Usher](https://x.com/thomasunise/status/2119605573234970966?a=OJImTm9AcHKss3ClTWE&s=20)‘s song “Confessions Pt. II,” a track about cheating. Another person created a [viral AI video](https://x.com/Byron_Wan/status/2119672475277840656?a=3IINs3Flwf4bBMcHWjs&s=20) that recreates the Coldplay concert where [a startup CEO was caught](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/19/astronomer-ceo-resigns-following-coldplay-concert-scandal/) on the Jumbotron hugging a colleague.

Huang publicly confessed to the affair just yesterday, so the internet has the rest of the week to get creative.

Yes, the internet is having a field day with this one.

As usual, AI-generated content is doing a lot of heavy lifting. Copyleaks, a software company that analyzes online content for signs of AI and digital manipulation, said Monday that it had seen “an explosion in AI-manipulated photos and videos of Huang and Alice Huang, ranging from memes to more subtle edits.” The tally includes at least 70 AI-related posts on X, which together drew around 8.42 million views and 64,362 engagements as of Monday morning. Those metrics have almost certainly climbed since.

## Summary

**TLDR:** The memes are funny, but the alleged Jensen Huang honeypot scandal exposes a serious problem: the AI economy's most important company has no public succession plan.

- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has allegedly been honey-trapped by a suspected Chinese spy, according to reports, and the story spread fast online.
- The allegation clashes with Huang's image as the steadiest of the AI leaders and a long-married family man.
- Nvidia is big enough that leadership drama alone is a market risk, and countless AI startups depend on its chips.
- Nvidia has no publicly disclosed succession plan, so it's unclear who would lead if Huang stepped down.
- The story also puts Nvidia's China ambitions under scrutiny. The memes are the light side of a governance question.
