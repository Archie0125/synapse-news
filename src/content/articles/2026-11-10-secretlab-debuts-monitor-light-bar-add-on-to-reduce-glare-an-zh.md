Popular [gaming chair](https://www.tomshardware.com/best-picks/best-gaming-chairs) and gaming desk manufacturer Secretlab has today unveiled its new [ErgoArch monitor lightbar](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1584492&xcust=tomshardware_us_7537301885411656178&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fsecretlab.co%2Fproducts%2Fergoarch-monitor-light-bar%3Fsku%3DMAG-MONLBAR-BLK&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tomshardware.com), a new lightbar attachment for owners of its Secretlab monitor arm and a complement to its desk ecosystem. Available now, the new lightbar costs $179.

For owners or prospective buyers of the Secretlab Magnus Monitor Arm (the light bar is an attachment to this accessory), the ErgoArch provides overhead lighting for your desk and monitor, reducing reflections and glare, and smoothing out brightness contrast between your display and workspace. Monitor light bars are generally designed to reduce eye strain while you're working or gaming, while also providing some snazzy ambient lighting; the ErgoArch is no different.

Secretlab says the ErgoArch provides both ultrawide illumination and focused task lighting depending on how you set it up. The lighting itself is smart and can be powered by Nanoleaf, giving you access via your smart home app to 16.8 million colors. There's also a dedicated wireless control puck, and the lighting temperature ranges from 2700K to 6500K.

The light itself is mounted to the monitor arm via a three-point adjustment arm and is compatible with any VESA-mounted monitor, including curved displays. There's also a magnetic webcam mount attachment. Power is provided by USB-C, so you don't need a wall outlet or extension socket to switch it on.

Available globally, the [ErgoArch is available to buy from Secretlab now for $179](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1584492&xcust=tomshardware_us_8441965136563753159&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fsecretlab.co%2Fproducts%2Fergoarch-monitor-light-bar%3Fsku%3DMAG-MONLBAR-BLK&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tomshardware.com).

## Summary

**TLDR:** Secretlab's $179 light bar is a well-built add-on, but it mainly serves to keep Magnus monitor arm owners buying from the same desk ecosystem.

- Secretlab has launched the ErgoArch monitor light bar for $179, available globally now.
- It only attaches to the Secretlab Magnus Monitor Arm, but it works with any VESA-mounted monitor, including curved displays.
- Its lighting uses Nanoleaf, offers 16.8 million colors through a smart home app, and has a color temperature range of 2700K to 6500K.
- It includes a wireless control puck and a magnetic webcam mount, and it is powered over USB-C.
- The light bar is part of Secretlab's move from chairs into a full desk-setup ecosystem.
