Spotify’s AI DJ, one of the company’s [earlier efforts in embracing AI technology](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/13/spotifys-new-dj-feature-is-the-first-step-into-the-streamers-ai-powered-future/) to connect people with their favorite music, is getting an upgrade. On Tuesday, the streamer announced that users will now be able to customize their DJ more like they can an AI assistant, by choosing between different voices and personalities. Those who like the feature, but not all the chatter, can also now opt for an experience with less commentary in between sets.

Since first launching the [AI DJ in 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/02/22/spotify-launches-dj-a-new-personalized-music-lineup-that-includes-ai-powered-commentary/), Spotify has leveraged AI across to improve all areas of its app. Most recently, that has included updates that allow [users to chat with a conversational AI agent](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/spotify-expands-its-ai-push-with-a-chatgpt-like-music-assistant/) about music, [fine-tune their recommendations with prompts](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/spotify-is-giving-you-the-keys-to-its-recommendation-algorithm-with-u-s-launch-of-taste-profile/), [create playlists with AI,](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/spotify-brings-ai-powered-prompted-playlists-to-the-u-s-and-canada/) and work with [third-party agents like ChatGPT](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/spotifys-current-ai-bet-is-about-being-the-music-service-that-agents-choose/) and Claude. However, the DJ remains one of Spotify’s most popular AI features, as it’s [used](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-05-07/dj-expansion-4-new-languages/) by over 94M Spotify Premium listeners.

Now, it’s revisiting one of its first major AI features, taking a cue from the personalization options other AI assistants routinely offer.

With the update, Spotify Premium subscribers will be able to choose from an expanded set of AI DJ voices in both English and Spanish. The voices include seven English options with British, American, Irish, Australian, and Indian accents, and two new Spanish options.

The different voices will have their own unique personalities as well. For instance, users can pick between three American voices, one of which is laid-back and warm (Casey), another that’s direct and has an edge (Tess), and a third that has a Southern accent and a youthful energy (Lane). In addition to the other various regional accents, there are also two new Spanish voices, one that’s more down-to-earth (Nico) and one that’s more witty and friendly (Fabi).

The voices will not replace the AI DJ’s original voice, “X,” which was based on that of a Spotify employee’s real voice recordings. However, DJ Fabi and Nico will now be the only Spanish voices available, replacing the earlier “Livi.”

The other change allows users to toggle off the commentary between songs, reducing the DJ’s interruptions. While typically, the AI DJ introduces different selections of music, explaining what it picked and why, users can now limit it to brief acknowledgements when they first start the DJ feature and when they turn it off. Spotify says this would allow people to use the DJ at times they may have turned to a playlist instead, like when hosting a party where they want music in the background, but not the chatter.

To access the DJ, users can search for the term “DJ” in the search bar of the Spotify app on their device, then tap the three-dot menu to pick their voice and commentary settings. To change genres, artists, or recommendations while the DJ is driving things, you simply tap the DJ button again.

Spotify says the new voices begin rolling out today, November 10, to Premium users in the markets where DJ is already supported. Currently, that includes over 95 global markets, where the DJ is supported in English, Spanish, [French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-05-07/dj-expansion-4-new-languages/).

## Summary

**TLDR:** Spotify's smartest AI DJ upgrade is letting you make it talk less, an admission that the best AI feature is sometimes knowing when to shut up.

- Spotify's AI DJ now lets Premium users choose between multiple voices and personalities, similar to customization in AI assistants.
- A new low-chatter mode reduces commentary between music sets for listeners who want the curation without the talk.
- The DJ is used by more than 94 million Spotify Premium listeners, making it one of the company's most popular AI features.
- The update sits within a broader AI push including a conversational music assistant, prompt-based recommendations, AI playlists and integrations with third-party agents like ChatGPT and Claude.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Spotify AI DJ 這次最聰明的更新，是讓它少說點話——承認最好的 AI 功能，有時就是懂得閉嘴。

- Spotify 的 AI DJ 現在讓 Premium 用戶從多種聲音與個性中挑選，客製化程度向 AI 助理看齊。
- 新增「少聊天」模式，減少歌曲之間的串場評論，滿足只想要選歌、不想聽廢話的使用者。
- AI DJ 已有超過 9,400 萬名 Spotify Premium 用戶使用，是該公司最受歡迎的人工智慧功能之一。
- 這次更新屬於 Spotify 更大的 AI 布局，包括對話式音樂助理、以提示詞調整推薦、AI 生成歌單，以及與 ChatGPT、Claude 等第三方代理整合。
