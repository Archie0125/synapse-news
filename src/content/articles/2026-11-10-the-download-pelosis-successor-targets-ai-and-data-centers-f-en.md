### **Nancy Pelosi’s replacement is ready to regulate AI**

*—Grace Huckins*

As a California state senator, Scott Wiener authored legislation aimed at curbing the risks of advanced AI. Now, after winning a tight congressional race for Nancy Pelosi’s open San Francisco seat, he could play a key role in setting federal AI policy.

I spoke with Wiener only a few hours after he had been declared the victor. But he was already thinking about AI risk: during our 20-minute conversation, he used the phrase “recursive self-improvement” half a dozen times.

“We need to slow this down,” he told me. “Because it’s moving very quickly, and we could sleepwalk into a disaster.” Still, he describes himself as only “cautiously optimistic” about Congress’s ability to rein in AI. ### **The backlash about data centers might really just be about electricity** 

*—James O’Donnell*

As voters went to the polls across the US last week, I was in Ashburn, Virginia, part of what’s known as Data Center Alley—the world’s largest data center hub. It’s also where the backlash sweeping the country arguably first began to take shape.

Big Tech has framed the data center uproar as a culture war over progress. But a few days in Ashburn convinced me the real fault line may be electricity: drawing it from the grid or generating it onsite provokes different forms of blowback.

The industry can improve its image, but it can’t escape the consequences of its enormous appetite for electricity. ## Summary

**TLDR:** Scott Wiener is bringing California-style AI safety politics to Congress, while the data center backlash turns out to be about electricity bills, not culture wars.

- Scott Wiener, who authored AI safety legislation as a California state senator, won Nancy Pelosi's open San Francisco House seat.
- He repeatedly raised recursive self-improvement and said 'we need to slow this down', but he calls himself only cautiously optimistic that Congress will act.
- Reporting from Ashburn, Virginia's Data Center Alley suggests the backlash against data centers is mainly about electricity.
- Drawing power from the grid and generating it onsite each provoke different kinds of opposition, which complicates Big Tech's progress-versus-luddites framing.
- Together, these signal that AI policy fights are shifting from abstract risk to local power and political capacity.

- 曾在加州參議院推動 AI 安全法案的 Scott Wiener，贏得 Nancy Pelosi 留下的舊金山眾議院席次。
- 他在訪談中多次提到「遞迴式自我改進」，直言「我們得放慢速度」，但對國會能否管住 AI 只表示「審慎樂觀」。

- 無論是從電網取電還是在現場自建發電，都會引發不同類型的反彈，打破了大型科技公司「進步對抗守舊」的敘事。
- 兩則新聞放在一起看，AI 政策之爭正從抽象的風險討論，轉向地方電力與政治實力的角力。
