Some Independent Games Festival (IGF) jurors published a statement yesterday saying they would not vote for games that use generative AI in “any capacity.” Soon after, the developer of indie hit *Big Walk* has published a statement saying it “sparingly” used AI in the “final stretch” of the game’s development to “help work through difficult technical challenges and bugs.”

# The indie game community is in disarray over AI and Big Walk

Big Walk’s developers said that they used AI ‘sparingly’ to help work through ‘difficult technical challenges.’

According to the [jurors’ statement](https://bsky.app/profile/austinwalker.bsky.social/post/3mzvyqaxnzc2w?ref=aftermath.site), while this year’s IGF entries have been “incredible,” there has been “a significant increase” in the number of games “developed with generative AI tools,” leading to “fairly intense discussion and disagreement” with no “universal consensus.” The statement includes a list of beliefs, including that generative AI has “negative effects,” that AI-generated code is “not an exception to these harms,” and that use of AI tools is “not a forgone conclusion in our industry.”

IGF jurors that signed include *Telling Lies* and *Immortality* creator [Sam Barlow](https://bsky.app/profile/mrsambarlow.bsky.social/post/3mzvzjbrkdk2g) and *Celeste* composer [Lena Raine](https://bsky.app/profile/lena.fyi/post/3mzvz5r72j22a). The jurors say that the statement doesn’t speak for IGF as an organization or for all IGF jury members, noting that some voiced concern about being able to safely sign “without professional blowback.”

One contentious title was [apparently *Big Walk*](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:fihtddt7ppe7m6hcspasv3go/post/3mzw663sctk2p?ref_src=embed), the [co-op adventure game](/games/973166/big-walk-review) from *Untitled Goose Game* developer House House. In a statement [to *Aftermath*](https://aftermath.site/big-walk-ai-igf-jurors-controversy/) and posted on [its website](https://bigwalk.game/faq/#ai), House House said that “In some cases, snippets from LLM responses were copied as a starting point to be modified into game code—we’d estimate that well under 0.1% of Big Walk’s code had anything to do with this usage, and generative AI was not used in any other context when making the game.”

House House said its “primary use case” of LLMs was “to research, via chatbots, issues we encountered with third-party code,” including fixing an issue with shadows by modifying LLM-suggested code. House House noted it turned to the “LLM chatbot” after researching the problem “extensively” and even speaking to an engineer at Unity.

The studio said it disclosed its AI usage as part of its IGF submission, though it has not disclosed AI usage on [*Big Walk*’s Steam page](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=87443X1797501&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fstore.steampowered.com%2Fapp%2F1478500%2FBig_Walk%2F) because “the platform does not request disclosure of code or chatbot usage.”

“This is not an endorsement of, or argument for, the use of AI tools, however minimal,” House House said. “We have some concern that voluntary disclosures like this might contribute to the normalisation of this technology, or its treatment as ‘inevitable’. We support the work of those who are trying to limit the harm generative AI is doing to videogames and our world, and apologise that Big Walk’s LLM usage may not meet their standards.”

House House didn’t have an AI policy in place when it developed Big Walk, which it described as a “failure,” and said it will “work to have a more comprehensive policy going forwards.”

## Summary

**TLDR:** The indie scene's AI purity test just hit its first beloved casualty, and 'we used it sparingly for bugs' shows how blurry the line has already become.

- Several Independent Games Festival jurors, including Sam Barlow and Lena Raine, said they won't vote for games that use generative AI 'in any capacity', and they explicitly include AI-generated code.
- The jurors cite a sharp rise in AI-assisted entries this year and say the statement doesn't represent IGF or the whole jury. Some jurors feared professional blowback for signing.
- House House, the studio behind Untitled Goose Game, said it used AI 'sparingly' in the final stretch of Big Walk to work through hard technical problems and bugs.
- Counting AI coding help as disqualifying sets a standard few studios can verify or meet, which turns awards into a fight over disclosure.
