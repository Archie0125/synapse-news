## Summary

**TLDR:** Medicare's WISeR program shows what happens when AI decides on care before anyone tests it: denials, delays and patients waiting in pain.

- In January, the federal government launched WISeR, a program that uses AI to evaluate Medicare beneficiaries' requests for certain procedures.
- EFF sued for records on WISeR and says documents show improper denials, operational chaos and long delays.
- EFF argues that treatment approval was outsourced to private AI companies without enough transparency, testing or legal limits.
- The same EFFector issue covers reverse-location warrants at the Ninth Circuit and Postal Service surveillance of online protest activity.
- WISeR could become a test case for how much algorithms should decide in public healthcare.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Medicare 的 WISeR 計畫示範了在沒人測試前就讓 AI 決定醫療的後果：錯誤拒絕、延誤，以及在痛苦中等待的病患。

- 聯邦政府在 1 月推出 WISeR 計畫，用人工智慧審核 Medicare 受益人申請的特定醫療程序。
- EFF 提告取得 WISeR 的相關紀錄，表示文件顯示有不當拒絕、營運混亂和長時間延誤等問題。
- EFF 認為政府把治療審核外包給私人 AI 公司，卻缺乏足夠的透明度、測試和法律限制。
- 同一期 EFFector 也討論了第九巡迴上訴法院的反向定位搜索令案，以及郵政署監控網路抗議活動的問題。
- WISeR 可能成為一個指標性案例，決定演算法在公共醫療中可以有多大的決定權。
