Over the past few years, X has released a number of features to make itself a friendlier hub for influencers. The latest effort to attract social media talent? Its own clipping tool.

Clipping is the [pivotal social media technique](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/29/social-media-follower-counts-have-never-mattered-less-creator-economy-execs-say/) by which longer videos are spliced into TikTok-attention-span length “clips” and then strategically posted to engage as many eyeballs (and thus derive as much ad revenue) as possible.

On Monday, X [announced](https://x.com/XCreators/status/2119892170224074832) the tool, which, from what we can tell, appears to be little more than a straightforward video-trimming feature that lets users edit videos and post them to X.

The site says that every clip includes a “Clip from” label that links back to the original video from which it was derived, allowing X users to see more about the creator and the creator’s content.

“Grab your favorite moment from any video, add your take, and post it without leaving X,” the company [posted](https://x.com/XCreators/status/2119892170224074832).

The platform added that both creators and clippers can “earn from views on the clip through Original Content Rewards,” X’s [rewards program](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards). The program is only available for eligible creators and pays out based on specific levels of audience engagement.

In many cases, influencers [pay special firms](https://www.forbes.com/sites/boazsobrado/2026/02/11/inside-the-clipping-farms-driving-fintechs-marketing-boom/) to handle clipping for them. More and more large companies are also [turning to such services](https://www.wsj.com/cmo-today/big-brands-venture-capital-and-mrbeast-put-money-behind-short-video-clipping-1e6ad58e) to run marketing campaigns, and the technique has been used [in political campaigns as well](https://www.theatlantic.com/politics/2026/09/conservative-hijack-social-media/688447/).

Over the last several years, X has tried to compete with other social sites by expanding the kind of content it supports. To take on sites like YouTube, X [introduced long-form videos](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/22/twitter-blue-users-can-now-upload-60-minute-long-videos/) in 2022 (when the site was still known as Twitter), and they have since taken off on the platform. To be more like Substack, it long ago dispensed with the site’s traditional character limits and introduced [long-form blog posts](https://www.engadget.com/you-can-write-long-form-articles-on-x-if-you-pay-for-premium-005707599.html), which are available to premium subscribers.

X has kept at it this year. In March, it added a feature called “[exclusive threads](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/x-revamps-creator-subscriptions-with-new-features-like-exclusive-threads-and-shareable-cards/)” that lets creators put parts of a thread behind a paywall so only subscribers can read them, and in July it launched a [livestreaming studio](https://x.com/XCreators/article/2072814635963560000) and set aside $1 million for creators who go live.

## Summary

**TLDR:** X's new clipping tool is a basic video trimmer, but it signals X wants to own the clipping economy that outside firms currently profit from.

- X launched a built-in clipping tool that lets users trim any video, add commentary and post without leaving the app.
- Each clip carries a "Clip from" label linking back to the original video and creator.
- Both creators and clippers can earn from clip views through X's Original Content Rewards, which is limited to eligible accounts.
- Clipping is now a big business: influencers, brands and even political campaigns pay specialized firms to do it.
- The feature itself is simple, so whether it works will depend on the payouts, not the editing tools.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** X 新推的剪輯工具技術上只是基本的影片剪裁，但它真正想做的，是把外包公司賺走的切片生意收回自己平台。

- X 推出內建剪輯工具，使用者可以直接在 App 內剪裁任何影片、加上評論並發布。
- 每段剪輯都附有「Clip from」標籤，連回原始影片和創作者。
- 創作者和剪輯者都能透過 X 的 Original Content Rewards 從觀看數分潤，但只有符合資格的帳號才能參加。
- 切片已經是一門大生意：網紅、品牌甚至政治競選都會付錢請專業公司代勞。
- 功能本身很陽春，能不能成功要看分潤是否有吸引力，而不是剪輯工具本身。
