### What to know about this year’s UN climate talks

Thousands of delegates, lobbyists, and journalists have gathered in Antalya, Turkey, for COP31, the annual UN climate talks. But this year’s negotiations are taking place against a particularly tense geopolitical backdrop, as the world falls further behind the Paris Agreement’s goals.

Energy prices are soaring, and there are big questions about how countries will tackle the climate crisis. Expect a lot of back and forth on fossil fuels and climate finance, and whether negotiators can agree on meaningful action.

*—Casey Crownhart*

## Summary

**TLDR:** COP31 must convert geopolitical friction into measurable climate action, while cognitive immortality remains a compelling thought experiment far ahead of demonstrated technology.

- COP31 has convened in Antalya as the world falls further behind the Paris Agreement’s climate goals.
- High energy prices and geopolitical tensions are making agreement on fossil fuels and climate finance more difficult.
- The negotiations will matter only if governments move beyond declarations toward enforceable policies, funding, and emissions reductions.
- The newsletter also promotes a discussion of cognitive immortality, including gradual brain replacement and cloud-based memory uploading.
- Those longevity concepts raise important identity questions, but the article presents them as speculative ideas rather than proven paths to survival beyond death.
