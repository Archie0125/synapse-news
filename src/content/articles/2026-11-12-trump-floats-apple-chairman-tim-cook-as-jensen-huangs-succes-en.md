In the wake of the industry-shaking news of [Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nvidias-jensen-huang-resigns-chipmaker-names-cfo-colette-kress-as-interim-ceo/) on Thursday, U.S. President Donald Trump is floating the idea that former Apple CEO Tim Cook should take over the position.

In a [post](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117604177898784579) on Truth Social, Trump wrote that “only one man” could take Huang’s place: Tim Cook of Apple.

“A Legend replaces a Legend!” he wrote.

Cook stepped down as Apple’s CEO on September 1, 2026, after [15 years running the company](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/21/apple-tim-cook-ceo-15-year-legacy-takeaways-ios-silicon-china-trillion-ai/), but he’s still very much involved with Apple. While [John Ternus took over as Apple’s CEO,](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/tim-cooks-parting-message-apple-is-in-the-hands-of-a-product-builder/) Cook transitioned into the role of executive chairman of Apple’s board of directors, which sees him continuing to engage with global policymakers and manage international relationships, including Apple’s relations with China.

Trump is not the only one to suggest Cook as a replacement for Huang. [Jeffrey Sonnenfeld](https://som.yale.edu/faculty-research/faculty-directory/jeffrey-sonnenfeld), Senior Associate Dean for Leadership Studies at Yale School of Management, who studies CEO leadership and corporate governance, penned [an op-ed in Time on Wednesday](https://time.com/article/2026/11/11/what-comes-next-for-nvidia/), suggesting Cook should be among the potential candidates. He wrote that Cook has “an unparalleled track record of succeeding a bigger-than-life founder,” in reference to Cook taking over from Apple founder Steve Jobs.

Whether or not Trump agrees with this opinion, or is simply blasting off an idea that hopped into his mind, is anyone’s guess.

“Tim Nvidia,” however, doesn’t quite have the same ring as [“Tim Apple” does](https://www.cnbc.com/2019/03/06/president-trump-calls-apple-ceo-tim-apple-instead-of-tim-cook.html).

## Summary

**TLDR:** Tim Cook has credible succession experience, but Trump’s endorsement is political theater until Nvidia’s board defines the job it actually needs filled.

- President Donald Trump publicly suggested former Apple CEO Tim Cook should replace Jensen Huang at Nvidia.
- Cook stepped down as Apple CEO on September 1, 2026, and now serves as executive chairman while John Ternus runs the company.
- Cook’s experience succeeding Steve Jobs and managing complex global supply chains makes the proposal more plausible than a purely celebrity-driven suggestion.
- His continuing responsibility for Apple’s international relationships, including China, could also complicate a move to a chipmaker facing national-security scrutiny.
- Nvidia’s board—not the president—will decide whether Cook or any other candidate fits the company’s technical and strategic needs.
