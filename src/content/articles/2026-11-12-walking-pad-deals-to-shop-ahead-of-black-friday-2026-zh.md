## The Foldable Walking Pad for $399 ($200 Off)

The [WalkingPad C2 Foldable Walking Treadmill](https://www.wired.com/review/walkingpad-c2/)’s track is 32.5 inches when folded (and unfurls to 57 inches), making it a great option for storage in small spaces. It weighs 62 pounds, which isn’t light, but it makes for a sturdier running deck and a (hopefully) longer lifespan. You can adjust the speed via the remote control or companion app. Reviewer [Kristin Canning](https://www.wired.com/author/kristin-canning/) found the app itself to be a little wonky. For example, if you don’t start a workout within the app, your data won’t sync. The app also saves each walking session separately, so if you take a break, you start from zero again. The good news is you don’t need to use the app in order to enjoy the walking pad.

Canning also didn’t like that when you first start using the machine, you have to walk at slower speeds before you can unlock the upper end of the range. This might appeal to folks who enjoy gamifying their workouts, though. The app also offers workout programs, which auto-adjust the speed to your pace via sensors under the belt.
