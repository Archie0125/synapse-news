*Written on November 13, 2026, covering news from Oct 26–Nov 1, 2026.*

Apple spread its hardware news across consecutive days this week, while Google and Mistral both sold new models on their cybersecurity skills but released them in opposite ways. Binance reported a theft estimated at about $6.3 billion, and OpenAI used a help-center page to merge ChatGPT and Codex usage into one meter for Plus, Pro and Business plans from December.

## Monday, October 26

Apple's new iPad mini moves to a bigger 8.5-inch OLED screen, runs on an A20 Pro chip, and is thinner and lighter than the model it replaces. It starts at $699 for Wi-Fi and $849 for Wi-Fi plus cellular, measures 5.4 mm thick and comes in two colors.

On the display side, Apple quotes peak brightness of up to 1,200 nits, which it says is more than twice the previous generation, plus 600 nits full-screen brightness and a 2 million:1 contrast ratio. A nano-texture option, previously offered on the iPad Pro, is now available, along with a 12-megapixel center stage front camera, faster wireless and cellular links and fast charging. Apple also described an "Acoustic Display" with woofers built into the screen.

Apple claims the 7-core GPU, with Neural Accelerators in each core, gives up to 4.5 times the AI performance of the last model, and that the 6-core CPU is up to 60% faster in multithreaded work. The tablet launches with the next generation of Apple Intelligence and Siri. Preorders open October 28 and availability starts November 6.

Minutes after the announcement, marketing chief Greg Joswiak teased an "exciting week". MacRumors said Apple was expected to show an M6 iMac, a 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro and MacBook Pro models with touch-enabled OLED screens, with a hands-on session for reporters and creators planned for Wednesday in Los Angeles.

## Tuesday, October 27

Apple unveiled the iMac with M6, offering up to a 12-core CPU and up to a 12-core GPU, each with two more cores than before, plus a Dual 16-core Neural Engine. Apple says it delivers up to 5x faster on-device AI than M4 and up to 18x the performance of the most popular Intel-based iMac. It adds Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6, an optional 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet connection available on models with the 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU, memory bandwidth up to 170GB/s and up to 2x faster storage. The 24-inch 4.5K display has an actual diagonal of 23.5 inches, and the machine comes in seven colors. U.S. pricing starts at $1,499 for the 10-core version and $1,699 for the 12-core version.

The 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 starts at $1,999 in space black and silver, with 24 hours of battery life, Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. Apple claims M6 graphics run as much as 30% quicker than M5, with AI workloads gaining up to 25%. Memory starts at 16GB and goes to 32GB, with bandwidth up to 170GB/s versus 153GB/s on M5. Ports include HDMI, MagSafe 3, three Thunderbolt 4 ports, an SDXC slot and a headphone jack, and with a compatible high-wattage adapter it can reach up to 50% charge in 30 minutes.

The National described the M6 as Apple's first 2-nanometre chip, announced in August, and noted that UAE prices of Dh8,499 for the MacBook Pro and Dh6,499 for the iMac match their predecessors after a July increase. It also noted the previous iMac, from 2024, used M4, with no M5 version. Shelly Goldberg, Apple's vice president of Hardware Engineering, said "iMac is the world's best all-in-one, and today it raises the bar yet again".

The same day, Apple announced AI tools for Creator Studio, due as a free update for existing subscribers in November. The National framed a touchscreen MacBook as the expected second major hardware revamp under new CEO John Ternus, after the iPhone Duo. Both Macs open for preorder October 28 and go on sale November 6, with iMac preorders in 35 countries and regions.

## Wednesday, October 28

Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, calling it its strongest model, with a focus on cybersecurity and long-horizon work. Google says it ranks first on version 5.0 of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with 59. Its cyber scores are 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on Collinear's CWE-bench, measured without guardrails. SecurityWeek reported these beat OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 in Google's comparison.

Carbon follows Gemini 4 Argon, launched two weeks earlier. Access starts this week with a subset of cyber defenders and will widen in phases to trusted partners through the Fairwind Program, which Google says has more than 650 participating organizations. Google says it is taking part in the U.S. government's voluntary pre-release testing process, with developer, business and consumer access promised "as soon as possible".

Carbon launches at Argon's introductory price: $2 for each million input tokens and $10 for each million output tokens. SecurityWeek reported those rise to $4 and $20 after the introductory period. On safety, Google reports a 0.0% attack success rate on Gray Swan's Indirect Prompt Injection (IPI) Benchmark against Argon's 0.7%, and a nearly 80% drop in high-severity misalignment alerts in internal coding use.

## Thursday, October 29

Mistral released the open weights of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk, a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model that entered public preview earlier in October. Hugging Face published its launch post the same day. INITE put active parameters at 49B per token while DataNorth cited about 52 billion; both list a 1M-token context window.

Mistral says additional reinforcement learning lifted Terminal-Bench 4.0 by 13 points to 41% while cutting output tokens by 30%, and that its 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index effectively ties GLM-5.3, using 47% fewer tokens per task on average. It reports 51% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index; CSO Online pointed out that Mistral's own chart shows DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and MiMo-V2.6-Pro scoring higher. Mistral says Large 4 turned down 96.6% of malicious cyber requests, which it calls the highest rate it has measured among open models.

The company was unusually direct about risk: after watching preview traffic, it wrote "we see a potential for uplift to conduct real attacks". Pierre Stock, Mistral's vice president of science, told CSO that "Subsequent safeguard updates are applied to our managed API and future model releases, rather than automatically updating downloaded checkpoints". A specialized security variant goes only to vetted design partners such as Orange Cyberdefense. Resultsense cited a Reuters report that GLM-5.3 completed 3 of 13 simulated network attacks in tests by Irregular.

The license is a modified MIT license allowing use, modification, fine-tuning and commercial use, but companies selling the model as a service need a separate commercial license. API pricing is $0.70 per million input tokens, $0.07 cached and $2.10 output, and DataNorth reported the EU endpoint costs 10% more. Microsoft is the first cloud provider to offer it, through Microsoft Foundry. Forrester's Charlie Dai said "Without a detailed technical report, the best open-weight model claim remains difficult to validate across workloads".

## Friday, October 30

Friday's reports put the estimated impact of the Binance hack at about $6.3 billion. According to Al Jazeera, Binance said on Thursday, October 29, that it had halted all deposits and withdrawals and that about $3bn had been frozen. Binance said an initial investigation pointed to unauthorized transactions made after a hacker got access to an engineer's compromised laptop, and pledged to cover all losses.

TRM Labs said it had high confidence that TraderTraitor, a hacking unit backed by the North Korean state, was behind the attack, and estimated about $6.36 billion left Binance wallets on 10 blockchains, most of it within roughly 20 minutes. Chainalysis CEO Jonathan Levin also pointed to TraderTraitor, putting the figure at $6.4 billion. Al Jazeera said the theft dwarfs the previous record, the $1.5bn Bybit heist in 2025.

Bitcoin traded below $78,000 on Friday according to CNBC, while Al Jazeera reported a drop of about 5 percent to below $77,000. Fundstrat's Sean Farrell estimated about $3.3 billion remained unfrozen and said the SAFU fund, worth about $1.2 billion, could cover roughly a third of remaining losses if frozen stablecoins are returned. TRM's Ari Redbord explained that "A freeze stops the money from moving, and recovery follows through legal process".

## Saturday, October 31

OpenAI's help article on usage limits, updated October 31, says Plus, Pro and Business plans will share one allowance across ChatGPT and Codex starting in December, measured in tokens. OpenAI says more advanced models and complex tasks generally draw more of the allowance, while Instant chat does not count. OpenAI wrote: "We expect most users won't need to wait for their usage to reset more often than they do now". It gave no exact date and no allowance sizes.

A separate help article that week said ChatGPT may block new purchases when too many subscriptions sit on one card or device, without publishing the limits. Free, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans do not count toward the device limit. Also on October 31, OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux posted that users can now switch between multiple accounts on mobile. Earlier, on October 29, OpenAI had reversed a planned Pro 200 cut and doubled Work and Codex allowances on Pro 100 and Pro 500.
