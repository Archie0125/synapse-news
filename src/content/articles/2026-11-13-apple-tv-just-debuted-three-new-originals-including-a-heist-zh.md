Apple TV subscribers can now watch three new originals, including a heist thriller series, a film based on true events, and the first of two holiday specials coming to the platform this season. Watch the trailers below.

Apple TV premieres sometimes overlap, giving subscribers multiple new titles to watch on the same day. Tonight, however, the platform is seeing a triple debut.

## Being Heumann

Up first is “[Being Heumann](https://apple.co/4jSRfqX),” which tells the true story of how Judy Heumann led a 1977 protest that resulted in the longest occupation of a federal building in US history.

The movie is directed by Siân Heder, whose previous film, “[CODA](https://apple.co/4mkbhbS),” earned Apple its first Academy Award for Best Picture, in addition to winning Best Adapted Screenplay for Heder and Best Supporting Actor for [Troy Kotsur](https://apple.co/3NCMxA3).

The premiere of “[Being Heumann](https://apple.co/4jSRfqX)” also marks the debut of [Apple TV’s new sign language accessibility feature](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/10/apple-tv-new-sign-language-accessibility/), which allows viewers to watch select titles with an American Sign Language (ASL) or British Sign Language (BSL) interpreter appearing in the corner of the screen.

In addition to “[Being Heumann](https://apple.co/4jSRfqX),” the feature is also available for “[F1 The Movie,](https://apple.co/4jFGPYe)” with “[Greyhound](https://apple.co/4sJKVn2)” and “[Way of the Warrior Kid](https://apple.co/3UQHP5i)” gaining support later this year. The latter premieres November 25.

## 12 12 12

The first two episodes of Apple’s anticipated heist thriller series “[12 12 12](https://apple.co/4zXsQWf)” are now available.

Starring Anthony Mackie ([Captain America: Brave New World](https://apple.co/3ZLteHN)) and Jamie Dornan ([Belfast](https://apple.co/4wwNiLS)), the series also features Patrick Fabian ([Better Call Saul](https://apple.co/3WO6JQA)) and Kali Reis ([True Detective: Night Country](https://apple.co/4nafQa1)), among others.

Here’s Apple TV on the new show, which will release new episodes every Friday through December 25:

Each episode of “12 12 12” unfolds across three timelines of a heist: the 12 months of planning, the 12 hours of the heist and the 12 days following it. The series follows a disgraced FBI agent (Anthony Mackie) and an American career criminal (Jamie Dornan) as they play a zero-sum game of cat and mouse across Europe. At the center of it all is the daring, epic raid on a bank vault deep beneath the streets of Zurich.

## Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy

The first of two holiday specials coming to Apple TV this season, “[Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy](https://apple.co/4jaCY7p)” is now also available to stream, the other one being “[Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift,](https://apple.co/3Wuqlwc)” which arrives December 4.

The premiere marks the latest release under Apple’s ongoing partnership with Peanuts, and joins a growing collection of original Peanuts productions, alongside classics such as “[A Charlie Brown Christmas](https://apple.co/49spW1L),” “[A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving](https://apple.co/4p9GBvt),” and “[It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown](https://apple.co/3VnYwW0).”

In fact, “[A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving](https://apple.co/4p9GBvt)” is free to stream on the platform this weekend, meaning even those without an Apple TV subscription can watch it.

Back to “[Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy](https://apple.co/4jaCY7p),” it follows Snoopy as he tries to get off Santa’s naughty list in the 12 days leading up to Christmas, learning along the way that “there’s more to Christmas than just getting gifts.”

## Summary

**TLDR:** Apple TV’s triple premiere matters less for volume than for making accessibility a product feature alongside prestige storytelling.

- Apple TV premiered three originals together: a heist thriller, a true-events film, and a holiday special.
- Being Heumann dramatizes Judy Heumann’s leadership of the 1977 occupation that became the longest takeover of a US federal building.
- The film launches Apple TV’s feature for displaying an ASL or BSL interpreter in a corner of the screen on supported titles.
- The first two episodes of 12 12 12, starring Anthony Mackie and Jamie Dornan, are available as Apple’s new heist-series offering.

## 摘要

**重點摘要：** Apple TV一次上架三部原創的重點不只是內容量，而是把手語無障礙功能與精品敘事放在同一個產品舞台。

- Apple TV同日推出三部原創作品，題材橫跨劫案影集、真人真事電影與節慶特別節目。
- 《Being Heumann》聚焦Judy Heumann領導的1977年抗議行動，該事件創下占領美國聯邦建築最久的紀錄。

- 由Anthony Mackie與Jamie Dornan主演的劫案影集《12 12 12》首播兩集，替這次上架補上商業娛樂性。
