Last week, Camp Snap launched a [miniaturized version](/tech/1027575/camp-snap-cs-8-mini-retro-film-camera-kid-friendly) of its [screen-free CS-8 video camera](/news/719103/camp-snap-cs8-digital-kodak-super-8-film-camer) that was half the size of the original with a smaller grip designed to be easier for kids to hold. But it turns out the company wasn’t done shrinking its hardware. Camp Snap’s new CS-8 Micro is even smaller than the CS-8 Mini. It’s around three inches long and weighs just 1.5 ounces so you can hang it from your keys and, like its larger predecessors, it can capture video using one of five built-in filters.

# The smallest version of Camp Snap’s retro digital camcorder fits on your keychain

The Camp Snap CS-8 Micro is just three inches long and adds a tiny LCD screen that’s only used as a viewfinder.

The CS-8 Micro is now available through [Camp Snap’s online store](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=87443X1797501&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.campsnapphoto.com%2Fcollections%2Fshop-all-products) and [Urban Outfitters for $39.99](https://click.linksynergy.com/deeplink?id=nOD/rLJHOac&mid=43176&murl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.urbanoutfitters.com%2Fshop%2Fcamp-snap-cs-8-blind-box-micro-digital-video-camera-keychain), and similar to the [collectible Kodak Charmera](/tech/949664/kodak-charmera-digital-camera-millennium-edition-collectible), the Micro is being sold in sealed mystery boxes. Colors like hot pink, lilac, lime, and orange will be easier to find, while vintage classic (black) and glow in the dark will be much rarer.

Unlike the original CS-8 and CS-8 Mini, the new Micro does include an LCD display, but it’s only used as a viewfinder. You can’t watch, edit, or delete anything you’ve captured on the camera itself. You need to transfer the videos to a phone or computer using a USB-C cable to enjoy them, or move its included 4GB microSD into a card reader.

As Camp Snap has been progressively shrinking its retro camcorder, it’s also reduced its resolution. The original captured videos at 4K, while the CS-8 Mini did 2.7K. The CS-8 Micro is limited to 720p, but carries forward features like an 8x digital zoom and a selection of five filters that include vintage effects that add flicker and grain to emulate the look of 8mm film. Camp Snap estimates the Micro’s 4GB microSD card can hold 45 to 6o minutes of video, while its battery life is estimated to be 30 to 45 minutes on a full charge.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Camp Snap’s keychain camcorder turns technical limitations into nostalgic charm, though mystery-box scarcity makes a playful camera feel unnecessarily collectible.

- The CS-8 Micro is about three inches long, weighs 1.5 ounces, and records video using five built-in filters.
- Its tiny LCD works only as a viewfinder; footage cannot be watched, edited, or deleted directly on the camera.
- Videos must be transferred through USB-C or by removing the included 4GB microSD card.
- The $39.99 camera comes in sealed mystery boxes, with common and rare colors creating a collectible sales mechanic.
