It’s been nearly a decade since Bethesda’s MachineGames released *Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus* in 2017, and now Xbox is ready to announce a sequel from the studio. *Wolfenstein 3* was officially announced today, just days after [Bethesda also confirmed](/news/1025273/the-elder-scrolls-vi-sentinel-name-announcement) that the next *Elder Scrolls* game will be called *The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel*.

# Xbox announces Wolfenstein 3

Xbox CEO Asha Sharma revealed a new entry in the Wolfenstein series with a brief tease.

In a post, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma announced the name and a photo of a script. “Looking forward to sharing more,” [she said](https://x.com/asha_shar/status/2121030203439010175?s=46). MachineGames also announced the game [in a post](https://x.com/machinegames/status/2121031100084453787?s=46), saying the game is “in production” and sharing a photo from a motion-capture session. IGN [reports that](https://www.ign.com/articles/wolfenstein-3-confirmed-to-be-in-development-at-machinegames) Brian Bloom (who plays protagonist William Joseph “BJ” Blazkowicz) and Debra Wilson (who plays Grace Walker) are reprising their roles, and they appear to be the featured actors in the photo.

The last mainline entry, *Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus*, launched in 2017 and continued the story of Blazkowicz in an alternate 1960s where Nazi Germany had won the Second World War. *Wolfenstein: Youngblood* also debuted in 2019 as a co-op spinoff, starring Blazkowicz’s twin daughters.

## Summary

**TLDR:** Wolfenstein 3's return is welcome, but a script photo and motion-capture tease are hype scaffolding—not yet evidence of a great sequel.

- Xbox and MachineGames confirmed that Wolfenstein 3 is in production, nearly a decade after 2017's Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus.
- The announcement consisted mainly of a script image and motion-capture photo, with no release date, platforms or gameplay details.
- Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson are reportedly returning as BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker.
- The project resumes the mainline series after the 2019 cooperative spinoff Wolfenstein: Youngblood.
