GPUs get the headlines, but connectors, cables and power racks are where AI data center limits show up, and TE Connectivity is cashing in.

## Key points

- At OCP Summit 2026, TE Connectivity showed three linked system-level racks: optical interconnect, a sidecar power rack and copper IT.
- TE's Digital Data Networks unit says it has grown massively over the past three years on the AI infrastructure buildout.
- The new VP of product management, Brian Payne, joined after 26 years at Dell, a sign that component makers are hiring for system-level thinking.
- TE argues that as AI clusters grow, high-speed connectivity and power delivery have to be designed together at the rack level.
- TE has been part of OCP since 2017, which ties it into open hardware standards for hyperscale data centers.

## Why it matters

In AI data centers, the cable is turning into the chip. Whoever moves electrons and photons between racks the fastest ends up with leverage that GPU vendors can't ignore.
